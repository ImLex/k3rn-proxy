====================================================================
 K3RN INTEL - CREW ONBOARDING (iPhone, fresh setup)
====================================================================

This gets your phone syncing HackEx captures to the crew tracker.
It's a one-time setup, ~15 minutes. After this it just works, on
both Wi-Fi and cellular.

You will do it in 5 stages:
  1. Install the K3RN Intel app
  2. Sign in and get approved
  3. Install WireGuard + import your personal tunnel
  4. Install and TRUST the security certificate  <-- most important
  5. Turn it on and confirm it works

--------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT YOU NEED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- An iPhone (iOS 15 or newer)
- The K3RN Intel app file (.ipa) - link in the crew channel
- The certificate file: k3rn-ca.pem - attached in the crew channel
- A free "WireGuard" app from the App Store
- 5 minutes of patience for the certificate step


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 BEFORE YOU START - ENABLE DEVELOPER MODE  (iOS 16 and newer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
On iOS 16+ a sideloaded app WILL NOT OPEN until Developer Mode is
on. Turn it on now so Stage 1 goes smoothly.

  1. Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > Developer Mode.
  2. Toggle it ON. The phone will ask to restart - let it.
  3. After it boots, unlock and tap "Turn On" on the popup, then
     enter your passcode.

  If you don't see "Developer Mode" in Privacy & Security, you're
  on iOS 15 - skip this, you don't need it.


====================================================================
 STAGE 1 - INSTALL THE K3RN INTEL APP
====================================================================
The app isn't on the App Store, so it's "sideloaded".

Easiest method (Sideloadly):
  1. On a computer, download Sideloadly (https://sideloadly.io).
  2. Plug your iPhone into the computer with a cable.
  3. Open Sideloadly, drag in the K3RN Intel .ipa file.
  4. Enter your Apple ID (used only to sign the app onto your phone).
  5. Click Start. When it finishes, the app appears on your phone.
  6. On the phone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management >
     tap your Apple ID under "Developer App" > Trust.

  NOTE: With a free Apple ID the app stops opening after 7 days.
  Just re-run Sideloadly to refresh it. (AltStore can auto-refresh
  if you'd rather set that up once.)


====================================================================
 STAGE 2 - SIGN IN AND GET APPROVED
====================================================================
  1. Open the K3RN Intel app.
  2. Sign in with Discord and authorize.
  3. Your account starts as "pending". Ping an admin in the crew
     channel to approve you.
  4. Once approved, you're a member. (You must sign in at least
     once before captures can be linked to you.)


====================================================================
 STAGE 3 - INSTALL WIREGUARD + IMPORT YOUR TUNNEL
====================================================================
The tunnel is your own private, personal connection - it only
routes HackEx traffic, nothing else on your phone.

  1. Install "WireGuard" (free) from the App Store.
  2. Back in K3RN Intel: go to the Settings tab >
     "Connect device (VPN)".
  3. Wait a couple seconds - it provisions YOUR tunnel.
  4. Tap the big "Push to WireGuard" button. This hands your tunnel
     straight to the WireGuard app.
  5. If iOS asks which app to open it in, pick "WireGuard".
  6. WireGuard opens ready to add the tunnel - give it any name
     (e.g. "K3RN") and save.

  (Prefer to scan a QR code instead? In K3RN Intel the tunnel screen
   shows a QR under "Prefer to scan?". In WireGuard tap "+" (top
   right) > "Create from QR code" and scan it. Either way is fine.)


====================================================================
 STAGE 4 - INSTALL AND TRUST THE CERTIFICATE  (DON'T SKIP)
====================================================================
This lets the crew server read HackEx traffic. Without BOTH the
install AND the trust toggle, nothing will capture.

IMPORTANT: open the certificate file in SAFARI or the Files app -
NOT inside Discord's built-in browser (that won't work).

  A) GET THE FILE ONTO YOUR PHONE
     - Download k3rn-ca.pem from the crew channel.
     - If it opened inside Discord, tap Share > Save to Files,
       then open it from the Files app.
     - You should see a popup: "Profile Downloaded".

  B) INSTALL THE PROFILE
     - Settings > General > VPN & Device Management.
     - Under "Downloaded Profile" tap "mitmproxy" > Install
       (top right) > enter your passcode > Install again.

  C) TURN ON TRUST  (this is the step everyone forgets)
     - Settings > General > About > scroll to the BOTTOM >
       "Certificate Trust Settings".
     - Toggle "mitmproxy" ON. Confirm.

  If you ever can't find the certificate: it does NOT capture until
  the trust toggle in step C is ON. Double-check that first.


====================================================================
 STAGE 5 - TURN IT ON AND CONFIRM
====================================================================
  1. Open the WireGuard app and toggle your "K3RN" tunnel ON
     (the switch turns blue/green). You'll see a small "VPN" icon
     at the top of your screen - that's normal.
  2. FULLY CLOSE HackEx if it's already open: swipe up from the
     bottom and swipe HackEx away. (This forces a fresh connection.)
  3. Open HackEx and log in / open your profile.
  4. Open K3RN Intel > Settings tab > pull down to refresh.
     Your in-game username, level, and stats should appear under
     "Game account". If they do - you're fully set up.

From now on, whenever the tunnel is ON, the targets you open in
HackEx sync to your personal tracker automatically - at home on
Wi-Fi or out on cellular, no difference.


====================================================================
 EVERYDAY USE
====================================================================
- Tunnel ON  = captures sync (leave it on while you play).
- Tunnel OFF = normal phone, nothing captured.
- You can leave the tunnel on all the time; it only touches HackEx
  traffic, so it won't slow down or route the rest of your phone.


====================================================================
 TROUBLESHOOTING
====================================================================
"My internet stopped working."
   You may have an old manual Wi-Fi proxy set. Settings > Wi-Fi >
   tap the (i) on your network > Configure Proxy > Off. We don't
   use a manual proxy anymore - only the tunnel.

"Nothing shows up in the app after playing."
   - Is the WireGuard tunnel actually ON? (VPN icon at the top.)
   - Did you do BOTH parts of Stage 4 - install AND the trust
     toggle in Certificate Trust Settings?
   - Fully close HackEx (swipe it away) and reopen it, so it makes
     a fresh connection.
   - Still nothing? Toggle Airplane Mode on for 10s, off, make sure
     the tunnel reconnects, then reopen HackEx.

"The app won't open / says it's untrusted."
   Free-signed apps expire after 7 days - re-run Sideloadly, or
   re-trust it under Settings > General > VPN & Device Management.

"HackEx logs me out or acts weird with the tunnel on."
   Turn the tunnel off, confirm the game works, then back on. If it
   persists, ping an admin - the server's HackEx pin may need a
   refresh.
